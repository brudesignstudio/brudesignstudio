# 📥 Drop your Bru brand assets in this folder

Put your files here using these **exact names** so I can wire them into the site
automatically. Don't worry about getting all of them — the logo and colors are what
matter most.

## Logo (please add)
- `logo.png` — your full Bru Design Studio logo (the one with the bulldog). Transparent
  background (PNG) is ideal. SVG is even better if you have it → name it `logo.svg`.
- `logo-mark.png` — just the **bulldog** on its own (no text), transparent background.
  This is the one I'll reuse as an icon throughout the site (nav, footer, section accents).
  SVG preferred → `logo-mark.svg`.
- `logo-white.png` *(optional)* — a white/light version of the logo for dark sections.

## Colors (please add — any ONE of these is fine)
- Easiest: just **type your hex codes** to me in chat, e.g.
  `primary #1A3D5C, accent #E0A437, dark #14110E`.
- Or drop a `colors.txt` / `palette.png` / brand-guide PDF in here and I'll read it.

## Fonts *(optional)*
- If your brand uses specific fonts, tell me the names (e.g. "Poppins + Lora") or drop
  the `.woff2`/`.ttf` files here.

## Anything else *(optional)*
- Photos of past work, a headshot/team photo, an icon set — drop them in and tell me where
  they should go.

---

**On a Mac:** open this `brand` folder in Finder and drag your files straight in, or
right-click an image in your browser → "Save Image As…" → save it here.

When you've added them, just tell me **"assets are in"** and I'll rebuild the site with
your real logo, the bulldog throughout, and your brand colors replacing the placeholder
gradient.
