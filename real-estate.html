The Residence

Where the canyon meets the horizon.

Set on a private bluff at the end of Marisol Point, this modern estate was conceived as a single gesture toward the Pacific, a floating pavilion of glass, board-formed concrete and fumed oak that dissolves the line between architecture and ocean. Every principal room opens to the water; every corridor ends in light.

The entire residence lives on a single sprawling level: a great room beneath nine-foot pivoting glass walls, a chef's kitchen with double islands, a wet bar, and a study easily converted into a seventh bedroom. A ten-seat cinema and a six-hundred-bottle cellar sit off the wellness wing, just above the four-car gallery garage carved into the bluff below. Five guest suites and a primary retreat with dual spa baths and a private ocean terrace complete the plan.

A home that doesn't ask for attention. It simply holds it.
